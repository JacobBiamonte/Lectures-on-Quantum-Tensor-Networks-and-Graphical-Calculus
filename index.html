01 Purpose

A common visual language for quantum information.

Tensor-network methods provide tools for understanding multilinear maps. Their graphical language represents states, channels, maps, and processes—bringing several strands of diagrammatic reasoning into one notation.

The lectures build from tensor fundamentals and Penrose graphical notation toward matrix product states, Boolean and stabilizer tensors, entanglement invariants, open quantum systems, and counting by contraction. The manuscript is intended to be self-contained and accessible to graduate students, while remaining useful as a research reference.

02 Lecture route

Seven parts.
One connected path.

The current manuscript moves from the grammar of tensor diagrams to applications of contraction. Each part adds a distinct layer of the formalism.

  1. 01

    From Tensors to Networks

    Foundations

    Penrose graphical notation, wire-bending duality, density operators, worked examples, and problems.

  2. 02

    Matrix Product States

    Factorization

    The diagrammatic SVD, matrix-product factorization of states, and numerical tensor-network algorithms.

  3. 03

    Boolean Tensor Networks

    Components

    Tensor-network representations of quantum states, COPY, XOR, and AND tensors, with worked network examples.

  4. 04

    Symmetries & Stabilizer Tensor Theory

    Structure

    Network symmetries, interacting Boolean tensors, the Clifford group, and Boolean stabilizer states.

  5. 05

    Tensor Networks & Entanglement Invariants

    Invariants

    Density-operator invariants, symmetries, polynomial invariants, and the route from binary forms to qubit states.

  6. 06

    Tensor Networks for Open Quantum Systems

    Representations

    Kraus, Stinespring, Liouville-superoperator, Choi-matrix, and process-matrix representations and transformations.

  7. 07

    Counting Solutions by Tensor Contraction

    Contraction

    Boolean quantum states, stabilizer tensor theory, elementary contraction theorems, and a three-fold way.

Appendix

Algebra on quantum states · XOR algebra · Karnaugh minimization · Tensors and tensor products
